Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

PeerSitter is built so there's as little as possible for a privacy policy to say: your video, audio, and recordings never reach any server we operate. This page exists because app stores require one — here are the specifics.

What PeerSitter does

PeerSitter connects a "Camera" device and a "Viewer" device directly to each other over an encrypted, peer-to-peer connection (WebRTC). Audio and video stream straight between those two devices. Recordings triggered by on-device motion detection are saved only in storage local to the device that made them.

Data we collect

We do not operate any server that stores, logs, or has access to your camera feed, microphone audio, or recordings. PeerSitter contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising SDK, and no account or login system — there is nothing tied to your identity for us to collect in the first place.

The signaling server

To introduce two devices to each other, PeerSitter briefly uses a small "signaling" relay to exchange connection-setup information: a randomly generated room ID and WebRTC connection parameters (SDP/ICE). This relay never receives your audio, video, or recordings, and the connection it helps establish is verified end-to-end so it can't silently intercept it either — see the architecture doc for exactly how. PeerSitter is self-hostable: if you run your own signaling server, you (or whoever operates it) control that infrastructure and its logs, not us.

Camera, microphone, and storage permissions

PeerSitter requests camera and microphone access to capture and stream video/audio, and local storage access to save motion-triggered recordings on your own device. These permissions are used solely for the app's core functionality and nothing captured through them is transmitted to us.

Recordings

Recordings are stored only on the device that captured or received them, in app-private storage. Nothing uploads automatically. You can play, export, or delete any recording from the in-app Gallery, and the oldest recordings are deleted automatically once your chosen local storage limit is reached.

Children's privacy

PeerSitter is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone — children or adults — because the app does not collect data at all.

Open source

PeerSitter's full source code — including this policy's source file — is public at github.com/lcsvcn/peersitter. You don't have to take our word for any of the above; you're welcome to read the code yourself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. The full history of any changes is visible in the repository's git log.
