Every device can be a camera or a viewer

Use any mix: an old Android phone as the camera, your Mac as the viewer, or the reverse.

Install in three steps

Pick your platform.

Then pair your devices

Pairing happens directly between your devices. The signaling relay only helps them find each other and never sees your video.

  1. On the device watching the room, choose Be a Camera.
  2. On your other device, choose Be a Viewer, then scan the QR code.
  3. Check that the fingerprint matches on both screens, and you're live.
